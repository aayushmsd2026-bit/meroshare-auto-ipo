    What it does by itself

    Every hour from 9 AM it checks MeroShare from this phone. On the last day of an IPO or FPO it applies for 10 kitta; for right shares, the full amount. Mutual funds and debentures are skipped. If the phone is offline, it applies as soon as the internet is back. You get a notification each time.
